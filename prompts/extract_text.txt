Rules:

- Preserve all code blocks with their original language tags.
- Preserve all tables, converting them to Markdown pipe-table format.
- Preserve all section headings using Markdown # syntax.
- Preserve lists (ordered and unordered).
- If legal notices, copyright statements, or license text appear, place them under a ## Legal section at the end.
- Do not add commentary, introduction, or conclusion text — only the document content.
